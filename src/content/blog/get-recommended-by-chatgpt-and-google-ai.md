More people now ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI Mode things like *"best interior designer near Ongole"* or *"who builds websites for small businesses in Hyderabad"*. These assistants answer by reading websites, listings and reviews. If they cannot find clear information about you, they recommend someone else.

No one can guarantee a mention. But these steps make your business easy for AI to find, understand and trust.

## Step 1: Say plainly what you do, where, and for whom

AI assistants quote clear sentences. Your home page should say in one line what you do, where you are and who it is for.

> **What we did:** Our own site opens with exactly that: websites, web apps and marketing from Hyderabad. For **Sunrise Interiors**, the site states clearly that it serves homes across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. [See Sunrise Interiors](/work/sunrise-interiors).

## Step 2: Answer real questions on your pages

People ask AI about process, timing and fit. If your site answers those questions in plain text, assistants can repeat your answer instead of guessing.

> **What we did:** We added a plain-language FAQ to our [How it works](/how-it-works) page and wrote detailed case studies for every project, so assistants have real answers to quote.

## Step 3: Add structured data

Structured data (schema.org) tells search engines your business name, location, contact details and services in a machine-readable way. It helps AI connect your website to the right business.

> **What we did:** Every page of our site carries business details, FAQs and case study information in structured data. ElastoMech's product pages are built the same way, with clear technical structure. [See ElastoMech](/work/elastomech).

## Step 4: Let AI crawlers in

Check that your `robots.txt` does not block crawlers such as GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot or Google-Extended. Some hosting and security settings block them by default.

> **What we did:** Our `robots.txt` explicitly allows the major AI crawlers, and we publish an `llms.txt` file that summarises who we are and what we have built.

## Step 5: Be on Google and Bing

Create and complete your **Google Business Profile**, and submit your site to **Google Search Console** and **Bing Webmaster Tools**. Several AI assistants rely on these search indexes.

## Step 6: Be consistent everywhere

Use the same business name, address and phone number on your website, Google profile, Instagram, Justdial and every other listing. Consistency builds trust with people and machines.

## Step 7: Collect real reviews

Reviews are one of the strongest signals for local recommendations. Ask happy customers to leave one on Google, and reply to every review.

## Step 8: Keep publishing useful answers

A short post that answers a real customer question gives assistants something to quote and link to. One good post a month compounds over time.
