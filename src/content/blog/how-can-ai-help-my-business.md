AI is not just for big companies. The most useful AI for a small business is boring in the best way: it answers customers when you are busy, finds people who need you, and does the repetitive work nobody enjoys.

Here are five steps, in the order we recommend, with what we built at each one.

## Step 1: Answer every customer question instantly

Most enquiries are the same ten questions. When nobody replies quickly, curious visitors leave and rarely come back. An AI assistant trained only on your own information can answer immediately, day or night.

> **What we did:** For **PropaTrade**, a trading education platform, we built a voice assistant into the website. Visitors talk naturally and hear answers about programs, plans and learning paths. Beginners felt more confident, and the support team stopped repeating themselves. [See the PropaTrade story](/work/propatrade).

## Step 2: Speak your customers' language

In India, your customers may prefer Telugu, Hindi, Tamil or English. AI can understand and reply in the language the customer chooses, which makes people far more comfortable asking questions.

> **What we did:** The PropaTrade assistant understands and answers in any language, so first-time visitors can ask in the language they think in. [See the multilingual assistant](/work/propatrade).

## Step 3: Find customers who are already looking for you

Your next customers are already describing their problem on LinkedIn, Reddit, Google Maps and Facebook groups. AI can read those signals and start a relevant conversation, instead of you paying for ads shown to the wrong people.

> **What we did:** We built a product-aware **AI lead agent** that understands a company's offering, detects buying intent across platforms and sends personal outreach. Sales teams spent time only on warm, qualified leads. [See the AI lead agent](/work/ai-lead-agent).

## Step 4: Automate the repetitive work

Look for tasks you do the same way every day: placing the same order many times, sending the same updates, copying data between tools. These are perfect for automation and free up hours every week.

> **What we did:** A trading educator had to place the same trade across many client accounts by hand. **TradingFloor** now executes one trade across every account at once, fast, with no monthly platform fees. [Read the TradingFloor story](/work/tradingfloor).
>
> At NIT Warangal, **ZeroX** automated order processing and inventory for campus stationery, raising the service provider's efficiency by 300%. [See ZeroX](/work/zerox).

## Step 5: Start small and measure

Pick one problem, the one that costs you the most customers or hours, and solve it with AI first. Measure the result for a month before adding more. Small wins build confidence and pay for the next step.
